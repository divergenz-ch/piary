What is the monthly payment on a 300 000 CHF mortgage at 1.8% a year over 25 years? Show the calculation so I can change the numbers.

<!-- pi -->

For a standard **amortizing mortgage**:

\[
M=P\frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n-1}
\]

Where:

- \(P=300{,}000\) CHF
- Annual rate \(=1.8\%\)
- Monthly rate \(r=0.018/12=0.0015\)
- Number of payments \(n=25\times12=300\)

\[
M=300{,}000\cdot
\frac{0.0015(1.0015)^{300}}
{(1.0015)^{300}-1}
\approx \boxed{1{,}242.31\text{ CHF/month}}
\]

Total paid: approximately **372,693 CHF**  
Total interest: approximately **72,693 CHF**

To change the numbers, replace \(P\), annual rate, and years in the formula.
