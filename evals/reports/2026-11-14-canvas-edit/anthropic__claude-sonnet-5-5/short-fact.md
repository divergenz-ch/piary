What does HTTP status 418 mean?

<!-- pi -->

HTTP 418 means "I'm a teapot". It comes from RFC 2324, the Hyper Text Coffee Pot Control Protocol, an April Fools' joke from 1998. A teapot that is asked to brew coffee responds with 418 because it refuses to do so. Some servers still use it as a playful or deliberate "I refuse" response, for example to turn away bots.
