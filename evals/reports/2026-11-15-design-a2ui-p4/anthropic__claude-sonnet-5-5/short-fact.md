What does HTTP status 418 mean?

<!-- pi -->

HTTP 418 means "I'm a teapot." It comes from RFC 2324, the Hyper Text Coffee Pot Control Protocol, an April Fools' joke from 1998. A teapot returns it when it is asked to brew coffee. Some servers still use it as a playful or deliberate refusal. It isn't meant for real use.
