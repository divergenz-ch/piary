What does HTTP status 418 mean?

<!-- pi -->

HTTP 418 means "I'm a teapot." It comes from RFC 2324, the Hyper Text Coffee Pot Control Protocol, an April Fools' joke from 1998. A teapot that is asked to brew coffee replies with 418 because it refuses. Some servers still return it, either as an Easter egg or to turn away unwanted requests. It isn't meant for real use.
