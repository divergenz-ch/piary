What does HTTP status 418 mean?

<!-- pi -->

HTTP 418 means "I'm a teapot." It comes from RFC 2324, the Hyper Text Coffee Pot Control Protocol, an April Fools' joke from 1998. A teapot returns it when someone asks it to brew coffee. It isn't a real error in normal use. Some servers return it on purpose, as an Easter egg or to turn away unwanted traffic.
