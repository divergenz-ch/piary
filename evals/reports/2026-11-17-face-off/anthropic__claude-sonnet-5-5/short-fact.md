What does HTTP status 418 mean?

<!-- pi -->

HTTP 418 means "I'm a teapot." RFC 2324 defined it as an April Fools' joke, the Hyper Text Coffee Pot Control Protocol. A teapot returns it when it's asked to brew coffee.

Real servers sometimes use it as an easter egg. Some also use it to turn away unwanted bots or requests. It isn't meant for production use.
