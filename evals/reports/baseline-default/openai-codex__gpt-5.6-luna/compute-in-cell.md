What is the monthly payment on a 300 000 CHF mortgage at 1.8% a year over 25 years? Show the calculation so I can change the numbers.

<!-- pi -->

Using the standard amortizing-loan formula:

\[
M=P\frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n-1}
\]

Where:

- \(P = 300{,}000\) CHF
- Annual interest rate \(=1.8\%\)
- Monthly rate \(r=0.018/12=0.0015\)
- Number of payments \(n=25\times12=300\)

\[
M=300{,}000\cdot
\frac{0.0015(1.0015)^{300}}
{(1.0015)^{300}-1}
\]

\[
\boxed{M\approx 1{,}242.45\text{ CHF per month}}
\]

This excludes insurance, taxes, fees, and any mortgage-specific Swiss amortization requirements.
